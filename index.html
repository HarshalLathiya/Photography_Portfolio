About

The Photographer

Lathiya Harshal

Photographer — Gujarat, India

I'm a photographer based in Gujarat, India. To me, photography is more than capturing images — it's about observing the world with intention and freezing moments that reveal raw beauty and authenticity.

My lens gravitates toward nature's quiet details, the untamed spirit of wildlife, the delicate geometry of flowers, and the unguarded truth in human expressions. I believe every frame should tell a story without words.
